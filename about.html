About Us



Yeshua's Grace Foundation
Yeshua's Grace Foundation (YGF) is a Non-Profit Company(NPC) dedicated to minimizing poverty,empowering generations, and enhancing the quality of life for those in need. Through strategic initiatives, sustainable programs, and community-driven efforts, we strive to create meaningful change that uplifts individuals and fosters long-term growth.
Our Vision
To build a world where poverty is reduced, opportunities are accessible to all, and every individual has the resources needed to thrive. We believe in sustainable solutions that empower people to break cycles of hardship and unlock their full potential.
Our Mission
At YGF, Our mission is to empower communities by providing essential services, creating opportunities for growth, and fostering long-term self-sufficiency. We are committed to supporting individuals through innovative programs, skill development, and resource accessibility, ensuring that every person we reach has the tools to build a better future.
What We Do
  • Community Support: Providing aid and essential resources to those in need.
  • Education & Skills Development: Offering programs that equip individuals with knowledge and practicals skills.
  • Empowerment Initiatives: Creating opportunities that promote independence and self-sustainability.
  • Innovation & Growth: Exploring new ways to drive social impact through sustainable strategies.
